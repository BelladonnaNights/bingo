FIX CATEGORY IMAGES

For every Bingo and Trivia category: finds every image link saved on it, tests which ones actually load, and saves them all together under the category's own ID as images: {banner, p45, l54, square}. A dead banner link is replaced by a working one from the same category. Nothing is overwritten with an empty value, and no link that works is ever replaced. Nothing changes until you press Apply.
